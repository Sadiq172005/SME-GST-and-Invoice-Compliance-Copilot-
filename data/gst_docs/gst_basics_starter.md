Section 16(2): ITC only if holder has a tax invoice/debit note, goods/services received, tax actually paid to government by supplier, and return filed.
Section 16(4): ITC for an invoice must be claimed by 30 November following the end of the financial year, or the date of filing the annual return, whichever is earlier.
Section 17(5): ITC is blocked for motor vehicles for personal/non-business use (with exceptions), food and beverages, outdoor catering, health/beauty services, club membership, rent-a-cab (unless obligatory), works contract for immovable property construction (other than plant & machinery), and goods/services used for personal consumption.
Rule 46: A tax invoice must carry a consecutive serial number of maximum 16 characters using alphabets, numerals, hyphen or slash.
Section 34: Credit notes must be declared by 30 November following FY end or annual return date.
